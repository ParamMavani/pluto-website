OUR PHILOSOPHY & ORIGIN

Browsing, reimagined.

Pluto was born from a desire for an unencumbered browsing experience that puts privacy, calmness, and simplicity first.

OUR JOURNEY

From a Private Search Engine to a Desktop Browser

Pluto did not start as a browser. It began with an ambition to create an alternative to surveillance-driven search engines: a clean, quiet search tool that delivered relevant web results without saving search queries, building advertiser profiles, or tracking user clicks.

The Initial Spark: Search Without Tracking

We started by prototyping an independent search interface designed to shield users from targeted advertisements and invasive query logging.

The Realization: Search Alone Isn't Enough

As development progressed, a fundamental flaw became obvious: even the most private search engine cannot protect users if the browser running it continuously collects telemetry, synchronizes tabs to corporate servers, and leaks browsing habits through background services.

The Evolution: Building Pluto Browser

To genuinely protect the user's journey, we had to control the entire window to the web. We pivoted to build Pluto as a dedicated desktop browser—integrating DuckDuckGo as default, keeping bookmarks and history strictly on your device, and crafting a tranquil cosmic visual identity.

✦
