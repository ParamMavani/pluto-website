OVERVIEW

Quick Start Guide

When you launch Pluto for the first time, you are presented with a serene, dark-mode cosmic start page. The omnibar address bar is focused and ready to explore.

  • Omnibar Navigation: Type any standard URL (e.g., github.com) to navigate directly, or type any phrase to search via DuckDuckGo automatically.
  • Navigation Controls: Use the top toolbar arrows to go back, go forward, and reload the current page.
  • Start Page Shortcuts: Quick access cards for your top destinations are displayed directly on the start screen.
WORKFLOW

Multi-tab Management

Pluto features lightweight, responsive tab switching designed to keep your workspace uncluttered.

  • Open a New Tab: Press ⌘ T or click the + button in the tab bar.
  • Close Tab: Press ⌘ W or click the close icon on the active tab.
  • Reopen Closed Tab: Press ⌘ Shift T to restore your most recently closed tab.
  • Switching Tabs: Click any tab in the top tab bar to bring it into focus instantly.

SECURITY

DuckDuckGo Search & Privacy

Pluto defaults to DuckDuckGo for all omnibar queries. DuckDuckGo does not store your IP address, record user search history, or share search queries with third-party data brokers.

In addition, Pluto's Electron browser shell enforces strict sandboxed renderer execution, blocks unsafe remote JavaScript execution, and disables diagnostic analytics reporting.

STORAGE ARCHITECTURE

Local Data & Storage

Unlike commercial browsers that upload telemetry, synced tabs, and browsing statistics to remote cloud servers, Pluto stores your data exclusively on your Mac.

macOS Storage Directory ~/Library/Application Support/pluto-browser/

Clearing Browsing Data: To wipe your cookies, cached assets, or browsing history, use the Clear Browsing Data option under the application menu or clear your local history from the History interface.

MACOS GATEKEEPER

Opening Unsigned Builds Safely

If macOS blocks Pluto from launching with a message that Apple cannot verify the developer, you can open it with a single right-click:

  1. In Finder, open your Applications folder.
  2. Hold the Control key and click the Pluto application icon.
  3. Select Open from the contextual menu.
  4. Click Open in the confirmation alert dialog.
