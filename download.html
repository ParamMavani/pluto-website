Notice macOS Gatekeeper Verification

Opening Pluto on macOS for the First Time

Pluto is an independent, community-driven open-source project. Because this build is currently unsigned and not notarized with an Apple Developer Certificate, macOS Gatekeeper may display a security prompt such as "Pluto can't be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software" or "Unidentified developer".

How to open Pluto safely:

  • 1
    Drag Pluto.app into your /Applications folder.
  • 2
    Right-click (or Control-click) on Pluto in Applications and choose Open from the context menu (do not just double-click).
  • 3
    On the macOS dialog box that appears, click the Open button. macOS will remember your decision, and Pluto will launch normally from then on.
  • 4
    Alternative: Open macOS System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Security section, and click Open Anyway next to the Pluto prompt.
